One image that sums up the world's news of Saturday 21 November 2026:
50 stories across 7 regions, every one of them from that Saturday.

## What is inside

- A masthead with the date, a strip of six key figures, and region counts.
- A UTC timeline of the day, from 00:00 to 19:00.
- Three big illustrated leads: Russian missiles land in Poland, COP31 slips
  into overtime in Antalya, and the S&P 500 falls into correction.
- Twelve numbered panels, each confirmed against its source, from the
  Kryvyi Rih double-tap strike to the Houthi missiles over Riyadh.
- A map of where it happened, a date-check box listing the stories left
  out on purpose because they are not from Saturday, and a numbered list
  of the remaining stories, 16 to 50.
- Sources are named at the foot of the image.

## How it runs

`main.py` is standard library only. It copies the pre-rendered `entry.png`
next to it into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`, after checking the PNG signature
and the SHA-256 of the file. It writes exactly the same bytes on every
run, with no network and no third-party packages.
